Minoan Motif Reception in Modern and Contemporary Textile and Fashion

This project explores the reception of Minoan motifs in modern and contemporary textile and fashion design through a digital image corpus and computational visualization.

The corpus brings together archaeological sources, historical design material, and contemporary fashion examples in order to examine how visual motifs circulate, transform, and reappear across different media and periods.

Project structure

The project includes images from different layers: archaeological material, printed and museum sources, and modern or contemporary design objects. The visualization allows the viewer to explore visual similarities, motif groups, and possible connections between objects.
